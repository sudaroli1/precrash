================================================================
WHAT THIS SETTLES, so you know why it is worth five minutes
================================================================

Four curves all cross the 0.5 threshold at frame 75. Three of them
score 1.10435, 1.10442, 1.10445 - agreeing to one part in ten
thousand. The fourth, a linear ramp, scores 1.06725.

That 0.037 gap is 2.24 frames' worth of score. Not 2, not 3 - 2.24.
So it cannot be explained by the ramp crossing at a different frame.
Something other than the crossing frame is being read, and the
paper's decomposition assumes nothing else is.

The five diagnostics all cross at frame 75 and differ only in shape:
graded before the crossing, graded after it, graded throughout, or
neither. Whichever one moves tells us what the scorer is reading.

The sixth is the organisers' own sample file, submitted verbatim. The
leaderboard shows a benchmark row at 0.58333 and everyone assumes that
is the sample submission - but our ramp, which crosses at the same
frame, scored 1.06725. One of those two beliefs is wrong.
